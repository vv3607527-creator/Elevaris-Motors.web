How we work

No shortcuts survive studio light

The same four stages on every car, whether it rolls in off a lease or out of a collection.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Paint depth is measured panel by panel and every defect is mapped under colour-matched studio light.

  2. 02

    Decontaminate

    Iron fallout, tar, old wax and bonded grit are stripped safely so nothing drags across the paint later.

  3. 03

    Correct

    Multi-stage polishing cuts until the finish reads flawless, then a jewelled finishing pass refines the gloss.

  4. 04

    Protect

    Ceramic is cured, edges are wrapped, trim is dressed and the warranty is logged before keys change hands.
